1. Introduction

NepaliPay ("NepaliPay", "we", "us", or "our") is operated by Fintex Australia Pty Ltd and provides a financial-and-social platform via mobile application (the "App"), customer support touchpoints, and a marketing website (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy describes the personal information we collect, how we use and share it, and your rights. We comply with:

  • the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs);
  • where you reside in the European Union or United Kingdom, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK-GDPR);
  • where you reside in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the CPRA;
  • other applicable data protection laws in jurisdictions where we knowingly operate.

By using the Services, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use the Services.

2. Important Clarifications

  • NepaliPay is a technology platform and is not a bank or licensed money transmitter. Certain regulated financial functions (card processing, stablecoin custody, payouts) are performed by third-party regulated providers identified in §6.
  • Public blockchains, where used for stablecoin transfers, are outside our control. Once a transfer settles on chain, it is generally irreversible.
  • This Policy describes our practices. Third-party providers maintain their own privacy notices that govern how they handle data they receive directly from you (for example, Stripe's card-data handling).

3. Information We Collect

We collect information in the categories below. Where a field is optional, we say so.

3.1 Account Information

  • Email address (required for email signup)
  • Phone number (required only if you sign up via mobile number; optional otherwise)
  • Username, display name, avatar, bio, country, gender (you provide these in profile setup; you control what is visible)
  • Date of birth (required at signup, used solely to enforce the 18+ age gate and applicable cash-game restrictions; we store only the date, not surrounding metadata)
  • Authentication identifiers from your chosen sign-in method (Supabase Auth, Sign in with Apple, or Sign in with Google)
  • Push notification tokens (Apple APNs / Google FCM) when you grant push permission

3.2 Identity Verification (KYC) Data

NepaliPay does not currently operate its own KYC programme. Where regulated providers (Stripe, Circle) require identity verification to process payments or stablecoin transfers, you provide that information directly to those providers; their privacy notices govern its handling. We may receive limited verification status references (pass / fail / pending) from those providers in order to enable or restrict features.

3.3 Financial & Transaction Data

When you transact through the Services we record:

  • Deposit, transfer, gift, game, and refund amounts and timestamps
  • Transaction status, internal references, and counterparty user IDs (where applicable)
  • Card metadata limited to brand, last 4 digits, and expiry month/year (we do not see or store full card numbers — Stripe does)
  • Stablecoin (USDC) wallet addresses and transaction hashes for transfers initiated through Circle

We never store full card numbers, CVC, or unmasked card PANs. Those are processed by Stripe.

3.4 Stablecoin & Blockchain Data

If you hold or transfer USDC through the Services:

  • Your developer-controlled Circle wallet address (a public blockchain identifier)
  • Outbound and inbound on-chain transaction hashes
  • Sanctioned-address screening results (we screen destination addresses against OFAC SDN and other sanctions lists before allowing a transfer)

Public blockchains are pseudonymous but persistent. Transactions you make on chain may be observable by anyone.

3.5 Messaging & Social Data

The Services include end-to-end encrypted direct messages and group chats, public community rooms, a social feed, calls, and gifts. We collect:

  • Direct-message ciphertext (we cannot read the content; encryption uses keys held only on your devices). Sender, recipient, timestamp, and message-status metadata are visible to us so we can deliver messages and compute unread counts.
  • Public community-room messages (these are not encrypted because the room is public; anyone in the room can read them)
  • Posts, comments, reactions, stories, and bookmarks you create on the social feed
  • Voice and video call records: caller, callee (or group conversation), timestamps, duration, and connection-quality telemetry. Call audio is encrypted end-to-end (DTLS-SRTP) and is not retained.
  • Gift events and balance toasts
  • Reports you file or that are filed against you

3.6 Device & Technical Data

Collected automatically when you use the App or website:

  • IP address and approximate country (derived from Cloudflare request headers)
  • Device model, operating system, app build version
  • A persistent device identifier (used solely as a rate-limit dimension and abuse signal)
  • Crash reports and error stack traces (forwarded to Sentry)
  • Anonymised product analytics events (forwarded to PostHog) — for example "deposit_started", "first_message_sent"
  • Authentication and security event logs (sign-in, MFA challenge, PIN change, session revocation)

3.7 Location Data

We do not continuously track your location. We collect:

  • Approximate IP-derived country (always — used to determine which regional features apply, e.g. NPR onramp restrictions for users in Nepal)
  • Precise GPS coordinates only if you (a) explicitly choose to share a location pin in a chat, or (b) are using a feature that explicitly asks for location at the moment of use

If you share a location pin in a chat, the coordinates are stored in the message metadata and persist for the same retention window as the message.

3.8 Customer Support Data

When you contact customer support (via the in-app help, email, or our website chat surface), we collect the contents of your message, any attachments you share, and metadata (timestamp, your account, the support agent assigned). Support tickets are retained per §8.

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Services
  • Authenticate you and verify session continuity
  • Process card deposits, internal transfers, and stablecoin operations
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and policy violations
  • Comply with legal obligations including AML / CTF, sanctions screening, and lawful information requests
  • Provide customer support and resolve disputes
  • Communicate service updates, transactional notifications, and (where you opt in) push previews
  • Conduct anonymised product analytics to understand which features are useful

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

6.1 Customer Support Access

Members of our customer-support team can, in the course of helping you with a ticket, view:

  • Your profile fields (username, email, country, ban status)
  • Your account balances and transaction history
  • Metadata about your direct messages — sender, recipient, timestamp, whether a message has been reported by another user. They cannot read the contents of your end-to-end encrypted direct messages or group chats. This is enforced architecturally: the encryption keys are held only on your devices.
  • Public community-room posts (these are public to all members of the room)

Higher-tier support and finance staff additionally see:

  • Refund history and the ability to issue refunds (with caps that depend on staff tier)
  • Ban history and the ability to ban or unban accounts

Every customer-support action is recorded in an append-only audit log. We will provide you with a record of who accessed your account on request, subject to identity verification.

  • the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (2021), incorporated into our DPA with the relevant provider;
  • the UK Addendum to the SCCs, where applicable;

We are progressively documenting Transfer Impact Assessments per provider; if you would like a copy of the assessment for a specific provider, contact us at the address in §14.

9. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information:

  • TLS for all data in transit
  • Encryption at rest for backend storage where applicable
  • End-to-end encryption (NaCl box, Curve25519 + XSalsa20 + Poly1305) for direct messages and group chats; the server cannot read DM contents
  • DTLS-SRTP for voice and video calls
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) on every database table containing user data
  • Append-only audit logging for customer-support actions and security-relevant events
  • Multi-factor authentication and session-token rotation
  • Idempotent transaction handling and atomic balance updates to prevent double-spend / double-credit

No system can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your device, biometrics, and PIN secure. Notify us immediately if you suspect your account has been compromised.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you reside, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you (via in-app data export or by contacting us)
  • Correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete
  • Erase your account and associated personal information (subject to AML/CTF retention as described in §8)
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent for processing based on consent (push previews, optional location, optional analytics)
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Australia, the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner; in the EU, your local data protection authority; in the UK, the ICO)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@nepalipay.com. We will respond within 30 days (GDPR / UK-GDPR), 45 days (CCPA), or as soon as practicable under APP. We may require identity verification before fulfilling a request to prevent fraudulent access. Subject-rights requests are recorded in an internal log so we can demonstrate compliance.

10.1 California Residents (CCPA)

California residents may additionally request information about the categories of personal information we have collected, sold, or shared in the prior 12 months. We do not sell or share your personal information within the meaning of the CCPA. You have the right to opt out of any future "share" via the contact in §14, and to non-discrimination for exercising any CCPA right.

10.2 EU / UK Residents (GDPR)

You may contact your local supervisory authority to lodge a complaint about our processing. Our lead supervisory authority for EU matters has not been formally designated; the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner is our primary regulator.

11. Children

The Services are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We require all account holders to confirm they are 18 or older during signup, and we collect a date of birth to enforce this. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13; if we discover that we have inadvertently done so, we will delete the account and any associated data without delay.

Cash-based games are restricted to users who are 18+ and are subject to additional regional restrictions where local law requires.

15. Definitions

  • Personal information / personal data — information that identifies you or could reasonably identify you, directly or in combination with other information.
  • Processing — any operation performed on personal information (collection, storage, transmission, use, deletion).
  • Controller — Fintex Australia Pty Ltd, which decides why and how your personal information is processed.
  • Sub-processor / processor — third party that processes personal information on our behalf under our instructions.
  • End-to-end encrypted (E2E) — content encrypted on your device with keys we do not hold; only you and the intended recipient can decrypt.
