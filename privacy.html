Privacy Policy

Proposed update for owner review — effective date pending approval

NepaliPay provides a mobile app, customer support, and a marketing website. This notice describes information used by those services. The availability of payment and wallet features can vary; the relevant data flows below apply when you use those features.

1. Information we handle

1.1 Account and profile

  • Information you provide to register and maintain an account, such as an email address or phone number, username, display name, date of birth, and optional profile content.
  • Sign-in identifiers and account security information used for authentication and abuse prevention.

1.2 Payments and activity

  • Transaction records, amounts, status, timestamps, and related account or counterparty references for features you use, including transfers, gifts, and store credit purchases.
  • If card deposits are available and you use them, Stripe handles card entry and processing; we handle the resulting payment and account records.
  • If Circle wallet features are available and you use them, wallet addresses, transfer instructions, and transaction identifiers are processed for those features. On-chain activity may be visible on a public blockchain.
  • Apple or Google may process an in-app store credit purchase on the corresponding platform. We receive purchase information needed to verify and credit it.

1.3 Messages, content, and calls

  • Messages and their delivery metadata, including sender, recipient or conversation, and time. The app encrypts private direct and group message content in supported flows. Public room content and message metadata are different from private message content.
  • Posts, comments, reactions, media, and other content that you choose to share. Content posted to public areas can be seen by other users.
  • Call participation and connection records needed to set up and operate voice or video calls.
  • If message previews are enabled, a short preview can be included in a push notification sent through notification providers. Device settings and app preferences affect what appears on the receiving device.

1.4 Device, usage, and support

  • Device and app information, IP address, notification tokens, security events, and usage information needed to run and protect the service.
  • Diagnostics and product analytics when the corresponding services are configured.
  • Information you provide when you contact support or report a problem.

2. Why we use it

We use this information to provide accounts, communication, payments and other requested features; deliver notifications; respond to support requests; maintain security; investigate misuse; and understand and improve service reliability.

3. Who receives information

Information is shared as needed for the feature you use. The source for the current app contains these integrations:

  • Supabase provides backend, authentication, database, storage, and related services.
  • Stripe processes card deposits when that feature is available and used.
  • Circle processes wallet and stablecoin operations when those features are available and used.
  • Apple and Google provide sign-in, app distribution, and platform purchase services where used.
  • Twilio delivers SMS or WhatsApp verification codes when phone verification is used.
  • Expo Push Service, Apple Push Notification service, and Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver push tokens and notification payloads when notifications are used.
  • Sentry and PostHog receive diagnostics or analytics events when configured.

Information you post in public areas is available to other users. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or to protect users and the service.

4. Security and retention

We use access controls and technical safeguards for information in the service. Private message encryption protects message content in supported flows; it does not cover account details, message metadata, public room content, or a notification preview that is sent to a push provider. No security measure can remove all risk.

We keep information for service, security, dispute, and applicable legal needs. Different records can have different retention periods. Account deletion may leave records that must be retained for those purposes. See Delete Your Account for the account deletion path, or contact us about a specific record.

5. Your choices and requests

You can update some profile details in the app, control available notification settings, and request account deletion. You may also ask to access, correct, or delete information, subject to applicable law and any records we need to retain.

6. Contact

For privacy questions and requests, email privacy@nepalipay.com.
