Privacy Policy

Proposed update for owner review — effective date pending approval

Fintex Australia Pty Ltd operates NepaliPay's mobile app, customer support, and marketing website. This notice describes information used by those services. The availability of payment and wallet features can vary; the relevant data flows below apply when you use those features.

1. Information we handle

1.1 Account and profile

  • Information you provide to register and maintain an account, such as an email address or phone number, username, display name, full name, date of birth, and optional profile content. Personal Details can also save a mailing address if you enter one.
  • Sign-in identifiers and account security information used for authentication and abuse prevention.

1.2 Payments and activity

  • Transaction records, amounts, status, timestamps, and related account or counterparty references for features you use, including transfers, gifts, and store credit purchases.
  • If card deposits are available and you use them, Stripe handles card entry and processing; we handle the resulting payment and account records.
  • If Circle wallet features are available and you use them, wallet addresses, transfer instructions, and transaction identifiers are processed for those features. On-chain activity may be visible on a public blockchain.
  • Apple or Google may process an in-app store credit purchase on the corresponding platform. We receive purchase information needed to verify and credit it.

1.3 Messages, content, and calls

  • Messages and their delivery metadata, including sender, recipient or conversation, and time. The app encrypts private direct and group message content in supported flows. Public room content and message metadata are different from private message content. If a message flow cannot use private-message encryption, do not assume that protection applies.
  • Posts, comments, reactions, media, and other content that you choose to share. Content posted to public areas can be seen by other users.
  • If you choose Location in a chat and grant foreground location permission, the app gets one position and sends its latitude and longitude in a location message to that conversation. It does not use this action for background tracking.
  • Call participation and connection records needed to set up and operate voice or video calls.
  • Some supported private-message flows attach a short preview to notification metadata. When the receiving user permits previews, that preview can be sent through notification providers. A notification preview is separate from encrypted message content.

1.4 Device, usage, and support

  • Device and app information, IP address, approximate country from request headers, notification tokens, security events, and usage information needed to run and protect the service.
  • Diagnostics if error reporting is available in your app. Product analytics is off until you opt in on your device; if analytics is available and you enable Product analytics in App Settings, events sent to PostHog can include a user identifier, feature activity, transaction amount and currency, gift credit values, or game stake and room identifiers.
  • If the GIF picker is available and you search it, the search terms are sent to GIPHY.
  • Information you provide when you contact support or report a problem.

2. Why we use it

We use this information to provide accounts, communication, payments and other requested features; deliver notifications; respond to support requests; maintain security; investigate misuse; and understand and improve service reliability.

3. Who receives information

The following services may receive information when you use features that rely on them. Availability can vary by device, account, and location:

  • Supabase provides backend, authentication, database, storage, and related services.
  • Stripe processes card deposits when that feature is available and used.
  • Circle processes wallet and stablecoin operations when those features are available and used.
  • Apple and Google provide sign-in, app distribution, and platform purchase services where used.
  • Twilio delivers SMS or WhatsApp verification codes when phone verification is used.
  • Expo Push Service, Apple Push Notification service, and Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver push tokens and notification payloads when notifications are used.
  • Sentry receives diagnostics if error reporting is available in your app. PostHog receives product analytics events only if analytics is available and you opt in on your device.
  • GIPHY receives GIF search requests when the picker is available and you use it.
  • When Ask AI is available and you use it, the messages in that AI conversation and context needed to answer may be sent to OpenRouter and its selected AI provider.

Information you post in public areas is available to other users. We may disclose information when required by applicable law or to protect users and the service.

4. Security and retention

We use access controls and technical safeguards for information in the service. Private message encryption protects message content in supported flows; it does not cover account details, message metadata, public room content, or a notification preview that is sent to a push provider. If a message flow cannot use private-message encryption, do not assume that protection applies. No security measure can remove all risk.

We keep information for service, security, dispute, and applicable legal needs. Different records can have different retention periods. Account deletion may leave records that must be retained for those purposes. Request account deletion through Me → Delete Account in the app, or follow the public deletion request guide if you cannot use the app. You can also email support@nepalipay.com. A request is not confirmation that deletion is complete.

5. Your choices and requests

You can update some profile details in the app, control available notification settings, and request account deletion. You may also ask to access, correct, or delete information, subject to applicable law and any records we need to retain.

6. Contact

Fintex Australia Pty Ltd handles NepaliPay privacy questions and requests. Email contact@nepalipay.com.
